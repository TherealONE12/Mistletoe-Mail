Mail.kicad_sym, Mail.pretty and models are a project-local selection
from the KiCad 10 symbol, footprint and 3D model libraries.
Copyright the KiCad library contributors and credited model authors.
Original metadata is retained in the STEP files.

The local symbols and footprints use shorter names and project-relative
references. The Pico H version omits the wireless module antenna keepout
and unused models. The port has bare pads. The LED uses the yellow model.
